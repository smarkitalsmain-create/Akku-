About

A commitment to growth, awareness, and service.

I am an educator and wellness practitioner whose work bridges the worlds of structured learning and holistic well-being. With experience teaching Social Studies, English, and Phonics to young learners, I bring patience, clarity, and genuine care to every classroom.

Beyond formal education, I have trained in Reiki healing and yoga — practices I view not as alternatives to conventional support, but as complementary tools for emotional awareness. My approach is grounded in the belief that true growth begins with self-understanding — recognizing the patterns, emotions, and energies that shape our responses to the world.

Having completed my B.A. (Hons) in Political Science in 2026, I maintain an active engagement with social issues and community development. My participation in forums like the Young Thinkers Forum at IIT Indore reflects my commitment to intellectual dialogue and meaningful contribution.

I work quietly, with intention — believing that awareness, not effort alone, is often the missing piece in personal and collective transformation.
